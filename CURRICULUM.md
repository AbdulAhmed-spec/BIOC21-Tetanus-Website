# Ontario Cells learning connection

Audience: Grade 7/8 students. Direct subject connection: **Ontario Grade 8 Science and Technology, Life Systems — Cells**. This is an introduction/support for Grade 7 learners, not a claim that organelles are a formal Grade 7 strand expectation.

| Learning goal | Student experience | Tetanus connection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain basic cell theory | Read the three ideas of cell theory and distinguish a bacterium from a body cell | C. tetani is one cell; its toxin is not a cell. The illustrated animal cell represents the host. |
| Identify cell structures and organelles and explain their functions | Select ten groups on the original labelled textbook drawing | Membrane and vesicle functions lead into chemical-messenger release. Normal functions are taught for all other structures. |
| Compare plant and animal cells | Compare shared parts with plant cell walls, chloroplasts and large central vacuoles | Human nerve cells are specialized animal cells, not plant cells. |
| Connect specialization with function | Learn why neurons have an axon and messenger-releasing endings | The toxin enters/undergoes transport through nerves and affects inhibitory-messenger release in the CNS. |
| Explain cell organization | Trace neuron → nervous tissue → spinal cord → nervous system → organism | A change in cell communication affects nervous and muscular tissue and whole-body movement. |
| Connect cell processes to physiological effects | Compare normal vesicle release with tetanus-blocked release | Reduced inhibitory output causes less braking of motor nerve cells and can cause stiffness/spasms. |

## Scope of the curriculum mapping

This table is a plain-language alignment of the new teaching content to the Grade 8 Cells topic. It is not a quotation of numbered provincial expectations or a claim to cover every expectation in that strand. Official Ontario curriculum pages were requested but returned proxy HTTP 403 in this environment. Exact expectation numbering and the current official wording were therefore not independently verified and are deliberately not asserted. The website links the official curriculum so instructors can compare the current document directly.

Official reference: <https://www.dcp.edu.gov.on.ca/en/curriculum/science-technology/grades/grade-8/b>.
